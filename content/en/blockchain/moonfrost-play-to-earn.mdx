## How Moonfrost Play to Earn Progression Works

The reported Moonfrost play to earn system is built around ordinary farming-game progression. New players cannot immediately collect meaningful rewards with starter equipment. They must first gather resources, improve their tools, expand their crafting options, and advance through the technology tree.

Players reportedly begin by picking up basic materials such as wood and stone. Those materials can be turned into simple tools, which then provide access to more advanced resources. The process follows a tiered structure: a stone pickaxe may occasionally uncover copper, while enough copper allows the player to craft equipment that can gather higher-grade materials.

This creates a gradual loop rather than an instant token faucet.
