Becoming a **Moonfrost Frost Hunter** is about more than gathering mysterious crystals: you must farm, craft, rebuild, and prove yourself to the Frost Hunters Association. This **Moonfrost Frost Hunter** guide explains the demo’s progression path, from making your first stone tools to completing the opening FHA exam rounds. Because the available evidence comes primarily from a demo playthrough, requirements and timings may change in later builds.

## What Is a Moonfrost Frost Hunter?

A Frost Hunter is a craft-focused adventurer who helps restore areas affected by frostfalls. These destructive events are connected to the second moon and leave Moonfrost behind, including enormous shards known as frost spires.

In the demonstrated village, a newly arrived frost spire signals that the recent frostfall has ended. This makes the surrounding area safer for reconstruction and Moonfrost gathering, giving hunters a reason to settle there.

Your early responsibilities combine several cozy-game systems:

- Collecting wood, stone, flowers, and other materials
- Crafting tools from blueprints
- Growing and harvesting crops
- Completing Frost Hunters Association requests
- Building and furnishing a home
- Refining basic resources
- Repairing damaged village infrastructure
- Taking the FHA membership exams

The story introduces these systems through Ash Davies and Walter Lemon. Ash supplies several important blueprints and guides you toward the FHA, while Walter introduces farming, seeds, and the primary early-game currency, called sill.

| Frost Hunter activity | Why it matters | Early example |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering | Supplies raw crafting materials | Pick up small rocks and fallen birchwood |
| Farming | Produces crops for requests and income | Grow radishes, followed by carrots |
| Crafting | Creates tools, furniture, and refined goods | Make a hoe, watering can, and birchwood planks |
| Reconstruction | Advances the restoration theme | Build a room and repair a broken bridge |
| FHA work | Provides rewards and supports progression | Submit requested crops and materials |
| Blueprint collecting | Expands what you can create | Unlock tool and decoration recipes |

Blueprints are especially important. The demo describes a hunter’s blueprint collection as a measure of their capabilities, and higher Frost Hunter levels appear to unlock additional recipes and tasks.

For official announcements and current game information, consult the [official Moonfrost website](https://moonfrost.io/). That is the best place to verify whether demo mechanics remain unchanged in the latest playable version.

## Best Early-Game Frost Hunter Progression Route

The opening hours introduce many activities in quick succession. Following the quests in their intended order reduces unnecessary walking and prevents you from spending resources before a task asks for them.

### 1. Gather loose wood and stone

Collect every small rock and fallen log you encounter near the farm and village. Gatherable objects periodically flash in the documented demo, making them easier to notice.

Do not assume that every mineral deposit can be broken immediately. Loose objects require no specialized tool, while larger stones, trees, and mineral nodes require the appropriate equipment.

It is worth building a small reserve before speaking to Ash. Early quests repeatedly consume birchwood and stone, especially when you begin constructing your home.

### 2. Craft the watering can and hoe

Ash provides the first blueprints and access to a basic workbench. Your initial equipment includes a stone watering can and stone hoe.

Use the hoe on suitable farm ground, plant seeds, and then water every occupied tile. Crops will not grow without water. The watering can can be refilled from a well or another accessible body of water.

A practical layout places the first crop plot near the farm’s well. In the referenced player experience, crops were initially planted farther away, creating unnecessary trips whenever the watering can emptied.

### 3. Start with radishes

Walter directs the player to buy radish seeds from a vending machine. A second seed machine is available near the farm, so you do not always need to return to the village store.

Radishes grew extremely quickly in the documented demo. However, this may reflect demo balancing rather than the final crop schedule, so do not rely on a specific growth time unless the current build confirms it.

| Early objective | Required action | Efficiency tip |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare farmland | Till soil with the stone hoe | Choose ground close to a water source |
| Plant radishes | Place seeds on tilled tiles | Begin with a manageable plot |
| Water crops | Use a filled watering can | Refill at the nearby well |
| Harvest produce | Interact with mature crops | Keep some crops until you check FHA requests |
| Earn sill | Submit requested items through the phone | Prioritize items you can replace easily |

Although one player bought 100 radish seeds, that is not necessarily the best opening purchase. A smaller batch leaves more sill available for later seeds or other expenses while you learn how much stamina your chosen plot consumes.

### 4. Unlock the core gathering tools

After completing the farming introduction, Ash provides blueprints for additional equipment. The observed order was:

1. Stone scythe  
2. Stone pickaxe  
3. Stone axe  

The scythe clears weeds and can expose useful flowers such as poppies. The pickaxe breaks weak stones and may yield resources such as copper, although stronger mineral deposits require upgraded equipment. The axe lets you chop suitable trees, including birch trees needed for construction and plank production.

This stage is where the **Moonfrost Frost Hunter** gameplay loop becomes clear: gather materials, use them to craft better items, and then access resources that were previously unavailable.

## Farming, Sill, Stamina, and FHA Requests

Farming provides more than food. It supplies materials for FHA requests, which are reconstruction orders accessed through the player’s phone.

In the recorded player experience, the FHA requested items such as radishes, rocks, birchwood, and red fabric. A radish submission produced a 300-sill reward, while another completed request awarded five star tickets. These examples should not be treated as fixed reward values for every build.

The same playthrough indicated that available requests refreshed approximately every five minutes. Because this observation comes from one demo session, current refresh intervals may differ.

### How to manage FHA requests efficiently

Open the request app before selling, refining, or using a large stack of materials. A common resource may be worth preserving when an active order offers a useful payout.

A sensible priority order is:

- Submit renewable crops first.
- Turn in loose stone or wood only if construction does not need it.
- Avoid consuming flowers before checking dye recipes.
- Keep spare birchwood for building and refined planks.
- Review new requests whenever the app refreshes.

Farming, crafting, mining, and chopping consume stamina. The demonstrated character had approximately 44.6 stamina during an early farming sequence, but this was a momentary player value rather than a confirmed universal starting statistic.

Sleeping restores stamina, and the demo allowed the character to wake once the meter had recovered—even during the night. Increasing the Frost Hunter level also appeared to raise maximum stamina and inventory capacity.

| Resource | Common early use | Recommended reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Birchwood | House walls, tools, planks, birdhouse | Keep a substantial stack |
| Stone | Tools, construction, FHA requests | Save enough for replacement tools |
| Radishes | FHA orders and early income | Submit surplus harvests |
| Poppies | Red dye production | Do not clear or spend all of them |
| Clay | Ceramic pots | Gather when preparing for exam round one |
| Moonfrost shards | Complex crafted objects | Reserve for advanced furniture |

Tool durability also matters. If an important tool is close to breaking, gather its replacement materials before exhausting it. This is particularly useful when preparing for a large woodcutting or mining session.

## Building Your Home and Raising Frost Hunter Level

After learning the primary gathering systems, Ash directs you to build a simple room. The demonstrated construction required enclosing a space with basic walls, adding a door, and placing a roof.

Avoid making your first room unnecessarily large. Every extra wall consumes resources, and the opening quest only needs a functional bedroom. You can expand after unlocking more materials and decorative options.

Walter then provides a single bed, bedside tables, and a decorations workbench. He also supplies an armchair blueprint, introducing the difference between basic tools and more complex crafted objects.

Simple equipment can draw on ambient Moonfrost particles according to the demo’s explanation. More advanced decorations require Moonfrost shards from your inventory. The leather armchair is the first prominent example.

Crafting and placing these progression items raises your **Moonfrost Frost Hunter** level. The interface explains that leveling can provide:

- More inventory capacity
- Higher maximum stamina
- New blueprints
- Additional tasks

The available recipes also include level gates. In the referenced build, some decorative options appeared at level 15, while stairs were shown with a level 20 requirement. These thresholds could change as development continues.

### Refined resources workbench

Completing the armchair task leads to a refined resources workbench blueprint. This station expands the available production options and enables materials such as:

- Birchwood planks
- Fabric
- Biofuel
- Red dye
- Blue dye
- Charcoal

Check the selected recipe before confirming a batch. In the recorded player experience, charcoal was accidentally crafted when birchwood planks were intended. Since exam and construction tasks can consume complete stacks, a mistaken production queue may create extra gathering work.

The refined resources workbench also marks a major progression point: constructing it opens the route to the Frost Hunters Association exams.

## How to Complete the FHA Frost Hunter Exams

The FHA exam tests whether you can apply the systems introduced during the tutorial. Rather than answering questions, you craft and place objects that demonstrate practical reconstruction skills.

Only three early rounds were shown in the available demo playthrough. The full exam, later requirements, and final license outcome were not documented, so this section should be treated as a partial walkthrough.

| Exam stage | Demonstrated task | Materials to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Qualification | Build a refined resources workbench | Resources shown by its blueprint |
| Round one | Craft and place ceramic pots | Clay and any listed recipe materials |
| Round two | Craft and place a birdhouse | Birchwood and red dye |
| Round three | Repair the bridge north of the farm | Rocks and birchwood planks |

### Round one: Ceramic pots

The first round asks you to create ceramic pots and place them. Clay is the key material, so gather it before formally beginning the crafting sequence if possible.

The demo player had to search for additional clay while finding copper instead. This illustrates why stockpiling several material types is more efficient than gathering only the resource required by your current tool quest.

### Round two: Birdhouse

The second round requires a birdhouse. Its demonstrated recipe used birchwood and red dye.

Poppies are relevant because the refined resources workbench can process materials into colored dyes. Clear weeds with the scythe, but pay attention to collectible flowers so that you do not overlook potential crafting ingredients.

Place the finished birdhouse when prompted. Merely completing its crafting timer may not satisfy the objective if the quest expects the item to be positioned in the world.

### Round three: Repair the bridge

For the third round, Ash directs you north of the farm to a broken bridge. Repairing it demonstrates a commitment to rebuilding the village and requires rocks plus wooden planks.

Prepare extra birchwood before this stage. Earlier tasks may remove or consume full material stacks, and planks must first be processed at the refined resources workbench.

This sequence captures the complete **Moonfrost Frost Hunter** progression loop: harvest raw materials, refine them, craft a useful structure, and restore access to part of the world.

## Moonfrost Frost Hunter FAQ

### How do you become a Moonfrost Frost Hunter?

Follow Ash’s introductory tasks, craft the basic tools, begin farming, build a simple home, and construct the refined resources workbench. That workbench opens the demonstrated FHA exam sequence, although the available playthrough does not show the exam’s final outcome.

### What should I collect first?

Prioritize loose stone and birchwood because early tools, construction projects, refined planks, and FHA requests use them repeatedly. You should also collect poppies for dye, clay for ceramic pots, and Moonfrost shards for advanced decorations.

### What is the best early way to earn sill?

Walter identifies farming as the main early income route. Grow radishes, check the FHA requests app, and submit requested crops or materials. One demo submission paid 300 sill, but rewards may be adjusted in other versions.

### Is the Frost Hunter License NFT required to play?

The supplied sources do not establish that an on-chain Frost Hunter Licence is required for gameplay. An OpenSea listing identifies a Moonfrost Frost Hunter Licence as an Ethereum ERC-1155 token with a displayed total supply of 500, but that listing alone does not prove a current gameplay requirement. Check official Moonfrost guidance before buying any digital item.
