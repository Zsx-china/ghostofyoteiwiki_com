## What Kind of Game Is Moonfrost?

Moonfrost combines farming, resource gathering, crafting, home construction, village restoration, character relationships, and a central mystery. Its setting is shaped by a supernatural disaster involving a second moon and recurring falls of icy meteorites known as Moonfrost.

Players take on the role of a Frost Hunter. These individuals can harness the strange substance left behind by the disaster and use its power to help rebuild a damaged world.

The premise gives familiar farming and life-simulation activities a larger narrative purpose. Growing crops, collecting resources, restoring buildings, and forming relationships all contribute to the recovery of a community living under the threat of another Frost Fall.
