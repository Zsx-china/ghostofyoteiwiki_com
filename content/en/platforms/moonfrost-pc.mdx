## What Is Moonfrost on PC?

Moonfrost is a single-player life simulation and RPG developed by Oxalis Games and published by Oxalis Games and iterco. It launched in Steam Early Access on October 12, 2026.

The story takes place roughly a century after a mysterious second moon appeared and caused recurring meteor storms. These meteors contain Moonfrost, a powerful material that certain people—known as Frost Hunters—can use to construct objects and restore damaged settlements.

As a Frost Hunter, you help rebuild a ruined village while gradually uncovering the truth behind the second moon and your character’s forgotten past. The adventure mixes several familiar cozy-game activities:

- Growing crops and gathering resources
- Fishing and cooking
- Crafting tools, furniture, decorations, and clothing
- Designing a home wall by wall
- Repairing village buildings
- Building homes for other residents
- Befriending villagers and developing relationships
- Exploring dangerous temples
- Improving Frost Hunting abilities

Its combination of farming, character relationships, construction, and fantasy exploration places Moonfrost PC between a traditional farming simulator and a story-driven RPG.

## Moonfrost PC Availability, Price, and Steam Features

The confirmed PC release is available through Steam. The store page also offers a downloadable demo, making it possible to test the game before purchasing the current Early Access build.

At the time this information was collected on October 13, 2026, Steam displayed a standard US price of $8.55 and an introductory price of $7.69. That 10% launch discount was scheduled to end on October 26, 2026. Prices and promotions can change, so check the [official Moonfrost Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2561630/Moonfrost/) for the amount shown in your region.

The following Steam features are currently listed:

- Single-player gameplay
- Steam Cloud
- Steam Family Sharing
- A free downloadable demo

- Steam community discussions for feedback and bug reports

No multiplayer or cooperative mode is listed. Anyone hoping to share a village with friends should therefore approach Moonfrost PC as a solo experience unless the developer announces a change.

The store information also does not confirm a native macOS or Linux release. Windows 11 is the only operating system named in the published minimum requirements, so users on other desktop operating systems should verify compatibility before buying.

## Moonfrost PC System Requirements

The published minimum specifications are modest in several areas, although the processor and graphics descriptions are broad. Steam lists only minimum requirements, not a separate recommended configuration.

| Component | Minimum requirement |
|---|---|
| Operating system | Windows 11 |
| Processor | 2 GHz |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM |
| Graphics | GPU with 3 GB video memory |
| DirectX | Version 10 |
| Storage | 2 GB available space |

The 2 GB storage requirement makes the initial installation relatively small compared with many modern PC games. However, players should leave additional free space for Early Access updates, save data, and possible future content.

There are also two important limitations to these specifications:

1. **The processor requirement is not model-specific.** A 2 GHz clock speed alone does not show how different CPU generations will perform.
2. **The graphics requirement only identifies video memory.** It does not name a particular AMD, Intel, or Nvidia GPU.

As a result, the published requirements cannot provide an exact frame-rate estimate for a particular computer. The free demo is the most practical way to evaluate performance on hardware near the minimum specifications.

Before installing Moonfrost PC, consider taking these steps:

- Update your graphics driver.
- Confirm that Windows reports at least 8 GB of installed RAM.
- Check that the active GPU has approximately 3 GB of video memory.
- Keep more than the listed 2 GB of storage free for updates.
- Test the demo at your preferred display settings.
- Verify that Steam Cloud synchronization completes before switching PCs.

Steam does not list recommended specifications in the supplied store information. Players seeking higher resolutions or smoother performance should therefore avoid assuming that meeting the minimum guarantees their preferred settings.

## What Is Included in Early Access?

According to the developer’s Early Access description, the main gameplay systems are already implemented, with dozens of hours of potential content. Nevertheless, the current build does not contain the complete main story or every friendship and romance event planned for version 1.0.

The initial Early Access release includes the first half of the main storyline. Players can also enter the first 20 temple rooms, restore multiple village businesses, meet 11 villagers, and raise current friendships to level 3.

Home design is another established part of the build. Players can construct and decorate their own residence, create homes for several villagers, and discover hundreds of furniture and decoration blueprints.

| Content area | Early Access state | Full-version plan |
|---|---|---|
| Main storyline | First half available | Complete main story |
| Temple exploration | First 20 rooms available | Additional content may arrive during development |
| Villagers | 11 can be met and befriended | More friendship content planned |
| Friendship progression | Currently capped at level 3 | All intended friendship events |
| Romance | Not all planned content is available | Romance options for all bachelors |
| Building | Player home and several villager homes | More locations and construction content may be added |
| Crafting | Hundreds of decoration and furniture blueprints | Collection expected to grow |

The developer intends to add the complete main storyline within six months of the Early Access launch. That is a development target rather than a guaranteed exit date. Oxalis Games has explicitly left open the possibility of remaining in Early Access longer if community feedback leads to additional work.

This distinction is important when deciding when to play. Someone primarily interested in construction, farming, fishing, crafting, and the available story may find plenty to explore now. A player who wants an uninterrupted narrative or complete relationship arcs may prefer to wait for version 1.0.

## Is Moonfrost PC Worth Playing During Early Access?

The answer depends on what you expect from an unfinished life simulator. Moonfrost PC already covers a wide range of activities, but some of its most important long-term elements remain incomplete.

Consider trying it now if you:

- Enjoy providing feedback during active development
- Want to experiment with detailed home construction
- Like alternating between farming, crafting, and exploration
- Do not mind friendship progression being temporarily capped
- Are comfortable reaching the current end of an unfinished story
- Want to use the demo to check performance first

Waiting may be the better choice if you:

- Only play stories after they are complete
- Want every friendship and romance event available
- Prefer finalized balancing and progression
- Do not want features or content to change
- Need clearer recommended hardware specifications
- Require confirmed support for an operating system other than Windows 11

Steam displayed a **Mixed** player-review rating when the source was collected, with 53% of 15 reviews marked positive. This is best treated as an early **player experience** snapshot rather than a dependable quality verdict. Fifteen reviews form a very small sample, and perceptions can change quickly as patches and new content arrive.

Early Access also means that bugs, balancing issues, and incomplete content are possible. The developer is using this period to gather feedback, and Steam directs players to its discussion boards for reports. If you choose to participate, describe bugs clearly by including what you were doing, whether the issue can be repeated, and any relevant system details.

For cautious buyers, the sensible order is straightforward:

1. Compare your computer with the minimum specifications.
2. Download the free demo.
3. Test performance and the core gameplay style.
4. Review the latest update history and community discussions.
5. Decide whether the current content is enough for you.
6. Confirm your regional price before purchasing.

That process is more reliable than judging Moonfrost PC solely by genre comparisons or its initial review percentage.

## Moonfrost PC FAQ

### Is Moonfrost PC available now?

Yes. Moonfrost entered Steam Early Access on October 12, 2026. It is playable as a single-player game, and Steam also offers a downloadable demo.

### Does Moonfrost have demanding PC requirements?

The published minimum requirements call for Windows 11, a 2 GHz processor, 8 GB of RAM, 3 GB of video memory, DirectX 10, and 2 GB of storage. Because no specific CPU or GPU models are named, testing the demo is advisable.

### Is the full Moonfrost story finished?

No. The Early Access version includes the first half of the main storyline. The developer plans to complete the story for version 1.0 and has targeted adding it within six months, although Early Access may continue longer.

### Can you play Moonfrost with friends?

No multiplayer or cooperative option is listed on Steam. The current Moonfrost PC release is identified as a single-player game with Steam Cloud and Family Sharing support.
