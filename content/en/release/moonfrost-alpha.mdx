## How to Follow Future Moonfrost Alpha News

The most dependable way to monitor development is to prioritize dated first-party announcements. Official terminology is especially important because “alpha,” “playtest,” “demo,” and “early access” do not necessarily describe the same build or access method.

The Moonfrost site includes news categories for Closed Alpha Testing, Game Development, Monthly Updates, and Early Access. Those labels organize the site, but their presence alone does not confirm that a test is currently accepting players.

Use this checklist before relying on an announcement:

- Confirm that the post comes from Moonfrost or Oxalis Games.
- Check the publication date and build number.
- Look for explicit registration or eligibility instructions.
- Verify whether access is open, invited, or limited.
- Check whether the announcement names supported platforms.
- Distinguish an alpha build from an event demonstration.
- Treat undated screenshots and social posts as potentially outdated.
- Avoid interpreting a site’s “Sign Up” link as guaranteed playtest admission.

| Information source | Best use | Key caution |
|---|---|---|
| Official Moonfrost news | Development posts and release announcements | Older posts may describe superseded builds |
| Official registration pages | Access requirements and application steps | Registration may not guarantee selection |
| Event recaps | Presentation details and attendee reactions | The event version may differ from tester builds |
| Community discussions | Player experience and informal observations | Claims require confirmation from official material |
| Third-party summaries | Convenient overviews | Check whether they link to the original announcement |
