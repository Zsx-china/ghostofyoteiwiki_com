## What Is Included in Moonfrost Early Access?

According to the developer, all principal gameplay systems are already implemented, and the current build offers dozens of hours of potential play. However, major portions of the narrative and relationship progression are not finished.

Moonfrost early access currently includes the first half of the main storyline. Players can also explore the opening 20 temple rooms, restore multiple village businesses, construct homes, and collect hundreds of furniture and decoration blueprints.

The current friendship cap is particularly important. You can meet and begin building relationships with 11 villagers, but progression stops at level 3 for now. The planned 1.0 release is intended to unlock the complete collection of friendship events and broader romance options.

## How Moonfrost’s Farming, Building, and Story Connect

Moonfrost takes place roughly a century after a mysterious second moon appeared and meteor storms began devastating the world. The icy material carried by those storms is called Moonfrost. Certain people, known as Frost Hunters, have learned to use its shards to create structures and restore damaged settlements.

The player takes on the role of one of these Frost Hunters. Rebuilding is consequently tied to both the setting and the main progression rather than functioning only as an optional cosmetic feature.

| Activity | Role in the game |
|---|---|
| Farming | Produces crops and supports the life-simulation routine |
| Resource gathering | Supplies materials for crafting and restoration |
| Crafting | Creates tools, furnishings, decorations, and customization items |
| Home building | Lets players construct and decorate a personalized residence |
| Village restoration | Reopens businesses and improves the damaged community |
| Relationships | Develops connections with villagers and supports character stories |
| Temple exploration | Adds danger and adventure outside the peaceful village routine |
| Frost Hunting | Connects magical abilities to rebuilding and progression |

The home-building system is more flexible than choosing from a small selection of premade houses. Players can design structures wall by wall, then furnish and decorate them. The same broader restoration theme extends to homes created for several villagers.

Character stories are also connected to the central mystery. As relationships develop, players learn about the fears, flaws, and histories of the village residents while investigating the protagonist’s forgotten past and the truth behind the second moon.

The intended result is a mixture of cozy routines and a darker fantasy storyline. Nevertheless, because only the first half of the main plot is available, Moonfrost early access cannot yet provide a complete resolution to those mysteries.

## Early Access Roadmap and Current Limitations

Oxalis Games says community feedback has influenced Moonfrost since its earliest builds. Early Access is being used to collect more feedback while refining the game and adding content.

The developer plans to add the remainder of the main storyline in less than six months. Since the game entered Early Access on October 12, 2026, that statement suggests a target within the first six months after launch, but it is not a guaranteed 1.0 release date.

The studio also warns that Moonfrost could remain in Early Access for longer if player feedback leads to additional development or more planned content. In other words, completion of the story and departure from Early Access should not automatically be treated as the same milestone.

Planned additions for the full version include:

- The complete main storyline
- All friendship events for villagers
- Romance options for all bachelors
- New gameplay features
- Additional areas to rebuild
- More villagers to meet
- Further character and world storylines
- Content introduced through Early Access updates

Several launch-version limitations deserve attention:

1. **The story stops halfway.** Players cannot currently experience the complete central narrative.
2. **Friendships stop at level 3.** Character progression is intentionally restricted.
3. **Romance is incomplete.** The full set of planned romance options belongs to a later version.
4. **Temple exploration is limited.** Only the first 20 rooms are identified as available.
5. **Future scope may change.** Early Access development can respond to feedback, making the final schedule uncertain.

No firm date for version 1.0 is provided in the available launch information. There is also no confirmed statement in the supplied material explaining whether the base price will increase after Early Access. Buyers should therefore avoid assuming that the current price or development schedule is permanent.
