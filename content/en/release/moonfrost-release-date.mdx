## What Is Included in Moonfrost Early Access?

Moonfrost combines relaxing life-simulation activities with an RPG story set in a damaged world. A mysterious second moon has caused recurring meteor storms, scattering an unusual substance known as Moonfrost across the land.

Players take on the role of a Frost Hunter who can use those fragments to build structures and restore the surrounding community. Alongside that central rebuilding mechanic, players can farm, fish, cook, collect resources, craft items, explore temples, and develop relationships with villagers.

Oxalis Games says that all of the main gameplay systems are already implemented. However, the narrative and social systems remain incomplete, with additional chapters, relationship events, and locations planned during development.

| Early Access feature | Current scope stated by the developer |
|---|---|
| Main story | First half is playable |
| Temple exploration | First 20 rooms are available |
| Villagers | 11 villagers can be met and befriended |
| Friendship progression | Currently capped at level 3 |
| Village restoration | Several businesses can be restored |
| Homebuilding | Players can construct and decorate a home |
| Villager housing | Homes can be built for several villagers |
| Crafting collection | Hundreds of furniture and decoration blueprints |
| Estimated content | Described as offering dozens of hours |

Housing is more flexible than choosing from a small number of preset upgrades. The Build Mode lets players design and decorate their homes wall by wall, whether they want a cottage, villa, or larger castle-like residence. Village restoration extends that construction system beyond the player’s property by introducing damaged streets, businesses, and homes to rebuild.

Crafting also affects both progression and visual customization. Available recipes include functional tools as well as furniture, decorations, clothing, and hairstyles. Players can therefore shape how they approach the game and how their character and property look.

The Early Access build includes the foundations of the social system, but friendships currently stop at level 3. Anyone primarily interested in complete character arcs or romance may want to wait for later updates, as the developer intends to expand friendship events and romance options before the full release.

### Is the Early Access Version Complete Enough to Play?

That depends on what you expect from Moonfrost. The existing build may suit players who enjoy open-ended farming, decorating, gathering, construction, and gradual community restoration. There are already multiple businesses to repair, 11 characters to meet, 20 temple rooms, and a substantial blueprint collection.

Story-focused players should approach it differently. Only the first half of the main narrative is currently available, so reaching the end of the existing chapters will not provide the complete resolution to the mysteries surrounding the second moon.

Early Access games can also change as developers respond to feedback. Progression balance, available content, and individual features may evolve before version 1.0. Players who prefer a finished narrative and finalized systems may receive a better experience by waiting.

## What Comes After the Moonfrost Release Date?

The developer plans to use community feedback throughout Early Access. That approach is intended to help Oxalis Games improve existing systems and decide whether additional material should be added beyond its initial plans.

According to the development notes, the team aims to add the entire main storyline in less than six months. However, this is not the same as announcing that version 1.0 will launch within six months.

Moonfrost could remain in Early Access longer if the team chooses to create more content in response to feedback. Players should therefore treat the story target as a development goal rather than a guaranteed full-release date.

The planned 1.0 edition is expected to include:

- The complete main storyline
- All friendship events for the villagers
- Romance options for all eligible bachelors
- More locations to restore
- Additional villagers and storylines
- New gameplay features introduced through Early Access updates

This makes the current Moonfrost release date the beginning of an ongoing development period. The most significant missing elements affect narrative completion and deeper relationships rather than the fundamental farming, crafting, building, and exploration loops.

Buying now makes the most sense if you enjoy participating in development, sharing feedback, and watching a game expand over time.
